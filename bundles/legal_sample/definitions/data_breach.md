# Definition: Data Breach

**"Data Breach"** (or **"Personal Data Breach"**) means a breach of security leading to the accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorized disclosure of, or access to, Personal Data transmitted, stored, or otherwise processed by Provider on Customer's behalf.

## Notification Obligations
Triggers the 48-hour emergency notice requirement under [Security Incident Notification](/contracts/dpa/clauses/security_incident_notification.md).

## Liability Supercap
Damages arising directly from a Data Breach are governed by the 2x [Liability Supercap](/contracts/dpa/clauses/liability_supercap.md) rather than the standard MSA limit.
