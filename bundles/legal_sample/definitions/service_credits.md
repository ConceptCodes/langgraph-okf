# Definition: Service Credits

**"Service Credits"** means fee percentages credited back to Customer's future invoices when Provider fails to achieve the contractual 99.9% Monthly Uptime Percentage under the [Service Level Agreement](/contracts/sla/index.md).

## Sole Remedy
Service Credits constitute Customer's sole and exclusive financial remedy for uptime failures, subject to the [Service Credits Clause](/contracts/sla/clauses/service_credits.md).
