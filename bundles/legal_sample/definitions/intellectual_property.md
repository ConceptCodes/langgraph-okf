# Definition: Intellectual Property

**"Intellectual Property"** or **"IP"** means all proprietary rights in and to intangible
creations of the mind, including:

1. **Patents** — Granted patents and patent applications covering inventions.
2. **Copyrights** — Original works of authorship, including software source code,
   documentation, and training data.
3. **Trade Secrets** — Confidential business information that provides a competitive
   advantage and is subject to reasonable protective measures (a sub-category of
   [Confidential Information](/definitions/confidential_information.md)).
4. **Trademarks** — Brand names, logos, and service marks.
5. **Database Rights** — Rights in compiled data collections under applicable law.

## IP Ownership Under the MSA

| Asset | Owner |
|-------|-------|
| CloudScale platform, software, and pre-existing technology | Provider |
| Customer's pre-existing data, systems, and branding | Customer |
| Customer Data uploaded to the platform | Customer |
| Aggregated, anonymised usage statistics | Provider (subject to DPA restrictions) |
| Jointly developed work (if any) | As agreed in the applicable Order Form |

## Relation to Other Clauses

- Breach of IP rights may constitute an uncapped liability under
  [Limitation of Liability](/contracts/msa/clauses/limitation_of_liability.md) Section 11.3
  if it involves [Confidential Information](/definitions/confidential_information.md).
- Third-party IP infringement claims are addressed through
  [Indemnification](/contracts/msa/clauses/indemnification.md) Section 12.1.
- Emergency injunctive relief for IP violations bypasses
  [Dispute Resolution](/contracts/msa/clauses/dispute_resolution.md) escalation
  under Section 15.4.
