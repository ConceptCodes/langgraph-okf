# Definition: Fees

**"Fees"** means the net subscription and platform usage fees under the applicable
Order Form. The governing clause determines whether the measurement includes
amounts paid or payable and the relevant measurement period.

## Exclusions
Fees explicitly exclude:
- Reimbursable travel or out-of-pocket expenses.
- Withholding, sales, or value-added taxes (VAT).
- Unaccrued potential future subscription periods.

## Usage in Liability Caps
Used as the multiplicative baseline for computing the [Liability Cap](/computations/liability_cap.md) under [Limitation of Liability](/contracts/msa/clauses/limitation_of_liability.md).

MSA 11.2 includes paid or payable fees in the 12 months preceding the first liability
event. [DPA 10.1](/contracts/dpa/clauses/liability_supercap.md) includes paid fees in
the 12 months preceding the incident. These totals can differ.
