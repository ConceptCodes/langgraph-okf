# Legal Definitions

- [Confidential Information](confidential_information.md) - Scope and exclusions for non-public proprietary materials.
- [Fees](fees.md) - Net invoiced amounts paid or payable under Order Forms.
- [Material Breach](material_breach.md) - Thresholds justifying immediate or cured contract termination.
- [Data Breach](data_breach.md) - Security incidents triggering mandatory regulatory and customer notifications.
- [Service Credits](service_credits.md) - Financial remedies for downtime under the Service Level Agreement.
- [Force Majeure](force_majeure.md) - Events beyond a party's reasonable control that excuse non-performance.
- [Intellectual Property](intellectual_property.md) - Patents, copyrights, trade secrets, and trademarks owned by each party.
- [Personal Data](personal_data.md) - Information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person under the DPA.
