# Definition: Material Breach

**"Material Breach"** means any substantial failure by a party to perform a fundamental obligation under this Agreement that substantially deprives the non-breaching party of the benefit reasonably expected under the bargain.

## Per Se Material Breaches
The following actions constitute Material Breach per se, without requirement of economic injury:
1. Failure to pay Fees exceeding forty-five (45) calendar days past due date.
2. Violation of the [Confidentiality Clause](/contracts/msa/clauses/confidentiality.md).
3. Willful infringement or reverse engineering of Provider Intellectual Property.
4. Failure to remedy security defects causing a [Data Breach](data_breach.md).

## Notice and Cure
Material Breach triggers a 30-day cure period after receipt of written notice under
the [Termination Clause](/contracts/msa/clauses/termination.md). That clause does
not specify a separate exception for incurable breaches.
