# SLA Section 2: Uptime Commitment

## 2.1 Commitment
Provider will use commercially reasonable efforts to make the CloudScale platform available with a Monthly Uptime Percentage of at least **99.9%** during each monthly billing cycle.

## 2.2 Exclusions
Monthly Uptime calculations exclude:
1. Scheduled maintenance announced at least 48 hours in advance.
2. Force majeure events or third-party Internet infrastructure failures.
