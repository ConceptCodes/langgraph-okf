# SLA Section 4: Service Credit Remedies

If Monthly Uptime falls below 99.9%, Customer is eligible for [Service Credits](/definitions/service_credits.md) applied against the next monthly invoice:

| Monthly Uptime Percentage | Service Credit Percentage |
| :--- | :--- |
| 99.0% to < 99.9% | 10% of monthly fee |
| 95.0% to < 99.0% | 25% of monthly fee |
| < 95.0% | 50% of monthly fee |

## Claim Requirement
Customer must submit a written credit claim within thirty (30) days of the end of the month in which the downtime occurred.
