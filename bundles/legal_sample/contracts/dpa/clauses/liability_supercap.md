# DPA Section 10: Data Protection Liability Supercap

## 10.1 Supercap Multiplier
NOTWITHSTANDING SECTION 11.2 OF THE [MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT](/contracts/msa/clauses/limitation_of_liability.md), EACH PARTY'S TOTAL AGGREGATE LIABILITY FOR ALL CLAIMS AND DAMAGES ARISING DIRECTLY OUT OF OR RELATING TO A [DATA BREACH](/definitions/data_breach.md) OR BREACH OF THIS DPA SHALL BE LIMITED TO **TWO TIMES (2X)** THE TOTAL [FEES](/definitions/fees.md) PAID BY CUSTOMER IN THE TWELVE (12) MONTHS PRECEDING THE INCIDENT.

## 10.2 Interaction with MSA General Cap
The supercap under Section 10.1 is an aggregate sub-limit and does NOT expand liability for general contract breaches outside the scope of data protection and privacy.
