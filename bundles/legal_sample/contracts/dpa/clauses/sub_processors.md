# DPA Section 9: Sub-Processors

## 9.1 Authorised Sub-Processors
Customer authorises Provider to engage third-party sub-processors to assist in
delivering platform services. Provider maintains a current sub-processor list at its
privacy portal, updated prior to any change taking effect.

## 9.2 Prior Notice of Changes
Provider shall give Customer **thirty (30) days' prior written notice** before:

1. Engaging any new sub-processor that will process [Personal Data](/definitions/personal_data.md).
2. Replacing an existing sub-processor with a new sub-processor.

The notice shall identify the sub-processor's name, country of operation, and categories
of Personal Data it will process.

## 9.3 Customer Objection Right
Customer may object to a proposed new sub-processor within **fifteen (15) days** of
receiving notice by providing written details of legitimate privacy or security concerns.
Provider shall work in good faith to address the concern.

If the parties cannot resolve the objection within thirty (30) days, Customer may
terminate the affected Order Form(s) without early-termination penalties, and Provider
shall refund pre-paid fees for the unused period.

## 9.4 Sub-Processor Obligations
Provider shall impose data protection obligations equivalent to those in this DPA on
each sub-processor by written contract. Provider remains fully liable to Customer for
acts and omissions of its sub-processors as if Provider had performed the processing itself.

## 9.5 Relation to Data Breach
A [Data Breach](/definitions/data_breach.md) caused by a sub-processor triggers
Provider's notification obligations under
[Security Incident Notification](security_incident_notification.md) and the
[DPA Liability Supercap](liability_supercap.md) applies in the same manner.
