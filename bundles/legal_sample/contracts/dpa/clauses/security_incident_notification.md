# DPA Section 6: Security Incident Notification

## 6.1 Notice Timeline
Provider shall notify Customer in writing without undue delay and, in any event, within **forty-eight (48) hours** of becoming aware of or confirming any [Data Breach](/definitions/data_breach.md).

## 6.2 Required Details
The notification shall include, to the extent available:
1. Description of the nature of the Data Breach including categories of records impacted.
2. The contact details of Provider's Data Protection Officer.
3. Likely consequences of the breach.
4. Remediation measures taken or proposed.

## 6.3 Breach as Material Breach
A failure to notify Customer within seventy-two (72) hours constitutes an incurable [Material Breach](/definitions/material_breach.md).
