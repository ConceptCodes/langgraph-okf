# Section 8: Confidentiality

## 8.1 Duty of Protection
The Receiving Party shall protect the Disclosing Party's [Confidential Information](/definitions/confidential_information.md) using the same degree of care it uses for its own proprietary information of like nature, but no less than reasonable care.

## 8.2 Permitted Use
The Receiving Party shall not use any Confidential Information for any purpose outside the scope of this Agreement and shall not disclose Confidential Information to any third party other than its authorized employees and contractors who have a need to know and are bound by confidentiality terms at least as restrictive.

## 8.3 Uncapped Exposure
Breach of this Section 8 constitutes an uncapped liability claim under [Limitation of Liability](limitation_of_liability.md) and represents a [Material Breach](/definitions/material_breach.md) per se.
