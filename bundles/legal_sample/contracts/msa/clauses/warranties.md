# Section 9: Warranties and Disclaimers

## 9.1 Provider Service Warranty
Provider warrants that during the applicable Order Form term:

1. The CloudScale platform will perform materially in accordance with the applicable
   documentation and the [Service Level Agreement](/contracts/sla/index.md).
2. Provider will implement and maintain reasonable technical and organisational measures
   to protect Customer Data as further detailed in the [Data Processing Addendum](/contracts/dpa/index.md).
3. The platform, as delivered, does not knowingly contain malicious code designed to
   disrupt, damage, or provide unauthorised access to Customer systems.

## 9.2 Mutual Authority Warranties
Each party represents and warrants that:

1. It has the legal power and authority to enter into this Agreement.
2. Execution, delivery, and performance of this Agreement does not conflict with any
   other agreement to which it is a party.

## 9.3 Disclaimer of Implied Warranties
EXCEPT AS EXPRESSLY SET FORTH IN SECTION 9.1, THE CLOUDSCALE PLATFORM IS PROVIDED
**"AS IS"** AND PROVIDER EXPRESSLY DISCLAIMS ALL OTHER WARRANTIES, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED,
INCLUDING BUT NOT LIMITED TO ANY IMPLIED WARRANTIES OF MERCHANTABILITY, FITNESS FOR
A PARTICULAR PURPOSE, AND NON-INFRINGEMENT.

## 9.4 Warranty Remedies
Customer's exclusive remedy for a breach of the Section 9.1 service warranty is:
(a) Provider's commercially reasonable efforts to correct the non-conformance; or
(b) if correction is not achievable within thirty (30) days, termination of the
affected Order Form and a pro-rata refund of pre-paid fees for the unused period.

A warranty breach not corrected within thirty (30) days constitutes a
[Material Breach](/definitions/material_breach.md) and triggers the cure and termination
rights under [Termination](termination.md) Section 14.2.
