# Section 15: Dispute Resolution

## 15.1 Escalation Requirement
Before either party initiates formal dispute proceedings, the parties shall attempt to
resolve the dispute through good-faith executive escalation. Each party shall designate
a senior representative who shall meet within **fifteen (15) business days** of written
notice of the dispute.

## 15.2 Mediation
If executive escalation does not resolve the dispute within thirty (30) days, either
party may request non-binding mediation administered by the American Arbitration
Association (AAA) under its Commercial Mediation Procedures. Costs shall be shared equally.

## 15.3 Binding Arbitration
Disputes not resolved through mediation within sixty (60) days shall be submitted to
final and binding arbitration administered by the AAA under its Commercial Arbitration
Rules. The arbitration shall be:

1. Conducted before a single arbitrator with experience in commercial software disputes.
2. Held in New York, New York (or by remote hearing upon mutual agreement).
3. Conducted in the English language.
4. Subject to the [Governing Law](governing_law.md) of this Agreement.

The arbitrator's award shall be final, binding, and enforceable in any court of
competent jurisdiction.

## 15.4 Exceptions — Injunctive Relief
Notwithstanding the above, either party may seek emergency injunctive or equitable
relief in a court of competent jurisdiction to prevent irreparable harm arising from
a breach of [Confidentiality](confidentiality.md) or [Intellectual Property](/definitions/intellectual_property.md)
obligations without first exhausting the escalation and mediation steps.

## 15.5 Relation to Material Breach
Initiation of a dispute does not suspend either party's obligation to perform, nor does
it extend cure periods under [Termination](termination.md) Section 14.2 for a
[Material Breach](/definitions/material_breach.md).
