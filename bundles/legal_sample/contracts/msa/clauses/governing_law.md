# Section 16: Governing Law and Jurisdiction

This Agreement, and any disputes arising out of or related hereto, will be governed by the internal laws of the State of New York, without regard to its conflicts of laws principles. The United Nations Convention on Contracts for the International Sale of Goods shall not apply.
