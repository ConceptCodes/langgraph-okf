# Section 11: Limitation of Liability

## 11.1 Consequential Damages Waiver
TO THE MAXIMUM EXTENT PERMITTED BY APPLICABLE LAW, IN NO EVENT SHALL EITHER PARTY BE LIABLE TO THE OTHER FOR ANY INDIRECT, PUNITIVE, INCIDENTAL, SPECIAL, OR CONSEQUENTIAL DAMAGES, INCLUDING LOSS OF PROFITS, REVENUE, OR DATA.

## 11.2 Aggregate Liability Cap
EXCEPT FOR THE EXCLUDED CLAIMS SET FORTH IN SECTION 11.3, EACH PARTY'S TOTAL AGGREGATE LIABILITY ARISING OUT OF OR RELATED TO THIS AGREEMENT SHALL BE LIMITED TO THE TOTAL [FEES](/definitions/fees.md) PAID OR PAYABLE BY CUSTOMER IN THE TWELVE (12) MONTHS IMMEDIATELY PRECEDING THE FIRST EVENT GIVING RISE TO LIABILITY.

See the sanctioned computational model in [Liability Cap Computation](/computations/liability_cap.md).

## 11.3 Excluded Claims (Uncapped Liabilities)
The limitations set forth in Section 11.1 and 11.2 shall NOT apply to:
1. Either party's breach of Section 8 ([Confidentiality](confidentiality.md)) involving [Confidential Information](/definitions/confidential_information.md).
2. Either party's gross negligence, willful misconduct, or fraud.
3. Customer's obligation to pay accrued and undisputed [Fees](/definitions/fees.md).

## 11.4 Relation to Data Processing Addendum
Pursuant to the order of precedence in the [MSA Summary](../summary.md), liabilities arising from a security incident or data protection breach are governed exclusively by the [Liability Supercap](/contracts/dpa/clauses/liability_supercap.md) in the Data Processing Addendum, which establishes an independent 2x cap.
