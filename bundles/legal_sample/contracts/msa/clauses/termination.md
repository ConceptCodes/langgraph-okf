# Section 14: Term and Termination

## 14.1 Term
This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues until all Order Forms have expired or been terminated.

## 14.2 Termination for Cause
Either party may terminate this Agreement immediately upon written notice if:
1. The other party commits a [Material Breach](/definitions/material_breach.md) and fails to cure such breach within thirty (30) days after receipt of written notice.
2. The other party becomes the subject of a petition in bankruptcy or any proceeding relating to insolvency, receivership, liquidation, or assignment for the benefit of creditors (immediate termination without cure period).

## 14.3 Termination for Convenience
Customer may terminate an Order Form for convenience upon sixty (60) days prior written notice, subject to payment of all remaining committed Fees through the end of the current term.

See detailed calculation in [Termination Notice Computation](/computations/termination_notice.md).
