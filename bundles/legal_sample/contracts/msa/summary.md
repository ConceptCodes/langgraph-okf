# Master Services Agreement (MSA)

This Master Services Agreement ("Agreement") governs Customer's access to and use of the CloudScale platform services. 

## Structure and Order of Precedence
In the event of any conflict between the terms of this Agreement and any addenda:
1. First priority: [Data Processing Addendum](/contracts/dpa/index.md) (solely regarding data protection and GDPR).
2. Second priority: Mutually executed Order Forms.
3. Third priority: The terms of this [Master Services Agreement](index.md).
4. Fourth priority: The [Service Level Agreement](/contracts/sla/index.md).
